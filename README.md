# Sales-dashboard-
Recommended Structure and Order 
1. Project Title & Headline
Project Title: Electronics & Hardware Sales Performance Dashboard (2020-22)
Headline: Empowering Data-Driven Retail Strategies Through Comprehensive Sales, Brand, and Team Performance Analytics.

2. Short Description & Purpose
Short Description: This Power BI dashboard provides a dynamic visual overview of technology hardware and electronics sales generated between the years 2020 and 2022. It serves as a single-screen analytical solution to track crucial business metrics such as total revenue, operational costs, profit margins, and overall sales volumes.
Purpose: The primary objective of this project is to analyze transactional sales data to deliver actionable insights for business owners and managers. It identifies top-selling product categories, determines market share distribution across different brands, and evaluates individual supervisor team performance to optimize inventory and streamline supply chain management.

3. Executive Summary / Overview Text
"The dashboard presented in the workspace utilizes a modern, dark-themed analytical layout tailored for high-level executive decision-making. By consolidating key performance indicators (KPIs) alongside core volume metrics, it offers an instantaneous health check of business operations. Seamlessly tracking sales teams and product performance side-by-side allows management to effectively pinpoint operational bottlenecks and capitalize on high-performing revenue streams."

4. Data Source & Key Metrics
Data Source: The dashboard is built upon a structured transactional database (such as a relational SQL database or compiled Excel/CSV data) consisting of comprehensive historical sales orders, cost prices, retail prices, product categorizations, brand classifications, and supervisor-to-team assignments spanning from 2020 to 2022.
Key KPI Metrics Displayed:
Total Sales: 99M
Total Cost: 76M
Total Quantity Sold: 13K
Transactions Count: 5K
Total Profit (Calculated): 23M (99M Sales - 76M Cost)

5. Key Features & Highlights
Top Performance Leaderboard (Supervisor Panel): The left section features an interactive profile panel for team supervisors (including Aadil Khan, Aarvi Gupta, Advika Joshi, Ajay Sharma, and Roshan Kumar), enabling management to filter the entire dashboard view by individual or team performance.
Product Demand Insights (Quantity by Category Wise): A clean horizontal bar chart effectively maps out product volumes, highlighting that Monitors (1.84K), Mice (1.50K), and CPUs (1.45K) are the highest-demanded items, while Printers and NIC cards reflect lower sales volume.
Market Share & Brand Dominance (Total Sales by Brand): An intuitive pie chart breaks down market share distribution, clearly showcasing brand dominance. Intel (18.89%) and Samsung (16.28%) lead the revenue generation, closely followed by Dell, Nvidia, and Western Digital.
High-Impact UI/UX Design: The executive dark background coupled with high-contrast color accents (Blue, Orange, and Purple) ensures superior data readability, reducing cognitive load and providing a highly polished, corporate aesthetic.
